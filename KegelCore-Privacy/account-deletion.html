Account & Data Deletion

Delete your KegelCore account.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — directly from inside the app, or by emailing us using the form below. Either way, the result is the same: a full, permanent erasure within 90 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Google sign-in identifier and all profile data we hold (email, display name, Firebase UID).
  • All onboarding answers (goal, age range, gender, fitness level, conditions, preferences).
  • All workout history, XP, streaks, level, programs, and goal-completion records.
  • Your subscription record on our side. (To cancel a paid subscription you still need to manage it in the Google Play Store — see step 3 below.)

Anonymised aggregate analytics that can no longer be traced back to you may be retained.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open KegelCore on your device.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to Settings.
  4. Tap Delete Account (shown in red).
  5. Confirm. You'll be signed out immediately and your data is scheduled for permanent deletion within 90 days.

If you change your mind during the 90-day window, email us at coderanil0@gmail.com and we'll cancel the request.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you don't have the app installed, send us an email and we'll process the deletion manually.

Email deletion request

Please send the email from the same Google account you used to sign into KegelCore so we can verify the request. We'll confirm receipt within 7 days and complete deletion within 90 days.

Option 3 — Cancel your subscription

Account deletion does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription (Google manages billing, not us). To stop being charged:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Tap KegelCore and then Cancel subscription.

You can also visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions in a browser.

Deletion timeline

  • Immediately: you are signed out of the app on every device. Your data is queued for permanent deletion and we treat the account as deactivated.
  • Within 90 days: we permanently remove your data from our active databases.
  • Within 90 days of that: the data is removed from encrypted backup snapshots as the snapshot rotation cycles through.
